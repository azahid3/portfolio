the role

As a Pre-Sales Solutions Engineer at Arrow Electronics, I support the Nutanix business across our partner ecosystem. My job sits at the intersection of engineering and revenue: architecting solutions for end customers, enabling partners to sell and deploy Nutanix, and building programs that remove friction from the sales cycle. Everything below is work I've personally led or built.

what i've delivered

unblocking supply chain with NC2 on the private marketplace

Situation: Hardware supply chain delays were stalling Nutanix deals. Partners had customers ready to buy, but long lead times on physical infrastructure meant stalled revenue and frustrated end customers.

Task: Find a path for partners to transact and deploy Nutanix without waiting on hardware.

Action: I led the initiative to bring the Nutanix NC2 product portfolio to the private marketplace, coordinating between our Nutanix and Microsoft teams in a joint REO effort. This meant aligning two vendor ecosystems internally so partners could transact NC2 on Azure directly through Arrow.

Result: Partners can now transact through Arrow with better pricing, direct access to a dedicated engineering team, and dramatically reduced time-to-business, deploying in the cloud in days instead of waiting weeks for hardware. This opened a new route to market for the entire NC2 portfolio.

building arrow's nutanix technical lab

Situation: Customers evaluating Nutanix needed hands-on experience before committing, but demo environments depended on vendor availability.

Task: Give Arrow its own environment for customer enablement, independent of vendor scheduling.

Action: I'm building out a Nutanix technical lab in our own environment: standing up the cluster, configuring workloads that mirror real customer scenarios, and designing guided enablement paths through the platform.

Result: Arrow can enable customers on Nutanix on our own timeline, letting prospects experience VM management, migration, and failover on live infrastructure before they buy.

30/60/90 partner enablement at scale

Situation: Partner engineers can't position or deploy what they don't understand. Certification paths existed, but partners weren't progressing through them on their own.

Task: Get the partner ecosystem certified and self-sufficient on the Nutanix stack.

Action: As part of a joint Nutanix/Arrow 30/60/90 program, I deliver 2-hour, one-to-many enablement sessions that take partners from the core NCA stack all the way through NCP-MCI.

Result: 100+ partners per session are actively progressing toward certification, multiplying the number of engineers in the channel who can independently sell, architect, and deploy Nutanix.

sizing & configuring customer solutions

Situation: An oversized quote loses on price; an undersized one fails in production. Every end-customer deal depends on getting the architecture right the first time.

Task: Translate each customer's real workload requirements into a right-sized, competitively priced Nutanix configuration.

Action: I gather workload profiles (compute, storage, IOPS, growth projections, failover requirements) and use Nutanix Sizer alongside hands-on judgment to configure clusters that fit both the technical need and the budget. Each configuration ships with the rationale documented so sales and deployment teams can defend and execute it.

Result: Right-sized solutions that hold up in production and in procurement, faster quote turnaround for partners, and fewer post-sale surprises for end customers.

what drives my work

business and technology must align

The best technical solution fails if it doesn't solve the business problem. The NC2 marketplace initiative worked because it fixed a revenue problem (stalled deals) with an engineering answer (cloud deployment).

enablement scales further than any single deal

One well-sized configuration closes one deal. One hundred certified partner engineers close deals I'll never even see. That's why I invest in the lab and the 30/60/90 program alongside deal-by-deal work.

relationships drive deals

In the channel, trust is the currency. Being the engineer partners can rely on, following through on demos and documentation, and advocating for the customer even when it complicates the deal, that's what builds long-term partnerships.
